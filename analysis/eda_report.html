10. Validation Strategy

  • GroupKFold(5): Duplicate measurement groups must stay in the same fold
  • Stratification: Maintain class proportions in each fold where possible
  • Metrics: Use F1 (Invalid class), BalAcc, ROC-AUC, MCC for classification; MAE, RMSE, R² for regression
  • Do NOT use accuracy alone due to class imbalance
  • Do NOT use Reference_Parameter for classification – it won't be available at test time

11. Risks and Leakage Warnings

  • Target leakage: Do not use Reference_Parameter as input for Validity_Label prediction
  • Test_ID leakage: Do not use Test_ID or its numerical part as a feature
  • Duplicate leakage: Duplicate rows must be in the same CV fold to avoid data leakage
  • Sensor_S4 missingness: High missingness may cause imputation to inject noise
  • Small dataset: Only 1000 rows and 134 Invalid – risk of overfitting, especially with many features
  • Cross-fitted residuals: Must be computed via out-of-fold prediction to avoid leakage
